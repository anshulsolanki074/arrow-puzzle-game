How To Play

1

Arrows Fly in Their Pointed Direction

Each tile contains an arrow pointing Up, Down, Left, or Right. Tapping it orders the arrow to fly off the board in that exact direction.

2

The Path Must Be Clear!

An arrow CANNOT escape if another arrow stands anywhere along its flight path. If blocked, it will bounce back and warn you of the obstacle.

3

Solve from the Outside In

Inspect outer perimeter arrows first. Once they fly away, they create clear escape corridors for the trapped inner arrows.

4

Earn Up to 3 Stars

Escape with zero blocked mistakes and beat the Par Time to earn all 3 Stars on every level!
